# 0001 — Record architecture decisions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-06

## Context

The rebuild spans many sessions and contributors (human and AI). Decisions that are not
written down get re-litigated or silently reversed.

## Decision

Every significant or product-owner decision is recorded as an ADR in `docs/decisions/`,
numbered sequentially from `0000-template.md`. ADRs are immutable once Accepted; a change of
mind is a new ADR that supersedes the old one.

## Consequences

`CLAUDE.md` links here. Decisions the product owner must make (money, pricing, retention) are
opened as `Proposed` and only become `Accepted` after explicit approval.
